Le problème
The problem

Votre travail peut être excellent. Votre acquisition peut rester imprévisible.

Your work can be excellent. Your acquisition can still be unpredictable.

Referrals are valuable, but they arrive when they arrive. For a studio or high-value service company, that dependency creates an invisible cost: the right clients do not discover you at the right time.
